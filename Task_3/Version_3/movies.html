Sinners

Sinners is a 2025 American horror film[a] produced, written, and directed by Ryan Coogler.[10] Set in 1932 in the Mississippi Delta, it stars Michael B. Jordan in dual roles as criminal twin brothers who return to their hometown in the Jim Crow South, where they are confronted by a supernatural evil. The film co-stars Hailee Steinfeld, Miles Caton (in his film debut), Jack O'Connell, Wunmi Mosaku, Jayme Lawson, Omar Benson Miller, and Delroy Lindo.

Prizes

"Sinners" won 229 out of 440 nominations in the 2025-2026 awards season, becoming the film with the most Oscar nominations in history, with 16 nominations.
